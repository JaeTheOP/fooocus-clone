# KLEVR 2.0.0 — Equipment Price Reduction

This release reduces all current customer-facing equipment prices by 10 percent.

## Included

- All catalog-controlled AJAX Tier 2 customer equipment prices are reduced by 10 percent.
- Current hardware add-on storefront prices are reduced by 10 percent when the hardware add-on table is available.
- Internal equipment cost remains unchanged.
- Monitoring, professional installation, pre-programming, shipping, tax, payment, and service fees remain unchanged.
- Historical orders and completed payments are not repriced.

The prior AJAX customer price represented a 30 percent markup over cost. Reducing that customer price by 10 percent results in a 17 percent markup over cost and approximately a 14.53 percent equipment gross margin as a percentage of the new equipment price, before processing, fulfillment, warranty, returns, and overhead.
